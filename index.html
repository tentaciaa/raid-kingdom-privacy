Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: October 17, 2026

Raid Kingdom is developed and provided by Tentacia ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game, contact us, or visit this privacy-policy website.

At a glance: No account is required. Game progress and preferences are saved on your device. The current game has no advertising or in-app purchases. Unity may process technical diagnostics. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send.

1. Information stored on your device

Raid Kingdom stores gameplay information locally so that your progress and preferences are available when you return. This includes:

  • Completed and unlocked levels, victory records, and best star ratings.
  • Campaign coins and purchased power upgrades.
  • Music and sound-effect volume settings.

These records are used to run the game and remember your choices. We do not operate a gameplay account system or upload these saved records to a Tentacia game server. Device or iCloud backups may include local app data according to your Apple settings.

2. Unity and technical diagnostics

The game is made with the Unity engine. With the current diagnostic configuration, Unity may process technical information such as an installation-specific identifier, device and operating-system information, IP address, and crash or error diagnostics. This information supports the functioning, stability, and security of the game and its underlying technology.

Unity Ads and Unity Analytics are not enabled in the current game. Engine diagnostics are separate from those services. For details about Unity's handling of information and privacy requests, see the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity Diagnostics privacy information.

3. Information you provide when contacting us

If you contact tentaciahelp@gmail.com, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any attachments or other information you voluntarily provide. We use this information to answer questions, investigate problems, and handle support or privacy requests.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information. Support email is handled through Gmail, subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising, tracking, and permissions

The current version of Raid Kingdom does not display advertisements, include in-app purchases, or use advertising identifiers to track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

The game does not ask you to provide an account, real name, phone number, or mailing address. It does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, or photo library for gameplay.

5. Service providers and disclosures

Information may be handled by providers that operate the technology described in this policy: Unity for engine diagnostics, Google for support email, and GitHub for hosting this website. Apple separately handles App Store downloads, Apple accounts, and any device diagnostics or backups you enable, under Apple's Privacy Policy.

We may disclose information we hold when necessary to comply with a legal obligation, respond to a valid legal request, protect people's safety or rights, or prevent fraud and abuse. We do not provide local gameplay records to a third party through a Tentacia server.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game data: Progress and preferences remain on your device until the app data is removed. On iPhone, deleting the app rather than offloading it removes its local app data. Data in device or iCloud backups may remain or be restored according to your Apple backup settings.

Support correspondence: We retain messages only for as long as needed to address your request, maintain necessary support records, or meet legal obligations. You can email us to request deletion, subject to any lawful retention requirements.

Diagnostics: Unity's Diagnostics documentation states a default retention period of 90 days for personal diagnostic data. Provider retention rules and applicable legal obligations may affect the information they retain. Contact us for help with access or deletion requests relating to the game; identifying the relevant installation may be necessary. Removing the app does not itself delete information already received by a provider.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

You can remove local game data by deleting the app and manage backups and diagnostic-sharing preferences through applicable device or provider settings. The game does not currently offer an in-game engine-diagnostics opt-out control.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information; obtain a copy; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent where processing relies on consent; or complain to your local data-protection authority. We will consider requests under applicable law and may need information to verify and locate the relevant records. We cannot directly access local saves on your device.

Where a legal basis is required, we rely on our legitimate interests in responding to support requests and maintaining a stable, secure game, to the extent permitted by law. We also process information when necessary to meet legal obligations. Contact us at tentaciahelp@gmail.com to ask about your information or exercise applicable rights.

8. Children's privacy

We do not ask players to provide personal details as a condition of playing. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information to us, including through support email, can contact us. We will review the request and delete information where required by applicable law. The technical service-provider practices described above also apply when the game is used.

9. Security and international processing

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information we handle, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protecting access to your device and backups helps protect your local game records.

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their privacy notices describe applicable processing locations and safeguards for international transfers. You can contact us for more information.

10. This privacy-policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, cookies, or external font services to this page. GitHub may process technical request information, including visitors' IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Links to third-party websites are governed by those providers' policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the game's features or information practices change. We will publish the revised policy here and update the date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before materially changing how personal information is handled.
